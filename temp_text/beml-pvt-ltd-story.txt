I joined BEML as Assistant Engineer in the
year 1984 from campus after completing
B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from IIT
(BHU). My professional career spans over
three decades,
working in various capacities in R&D,
Technology division and manufacturing
functions like planning, procurement,
production, inspection & testing including
Business Development, Finance and
HR.Under Rail & Metro vertical the products
developed are Metro cars, EMUs (Electrical
Multiple Units) and Maintenance vehicles.
My significant contributions include
establishing the indigenous design,
engineering and testing capability for metro
rolling stock involving sub-Systems like car-
body, bogie, brake, outfitting, propulsion
&amp; controls, interfacing with signalling,
telecom, track, power supply, etc.
Developed indigenous vendor base for
critical metro car
aggregates.As a young engineer, I was
actively involved in development of
Defense Products like Pontoon floating
bridge system (PMS Bridge), heavy-duty
trucks for carrying missiles, tank
transportation trailer, special wagons and
ground support equipment like Aircraft
towing tractor & Weapon Loader.
 Nation’s First Stainless Steel Electrical

Railways for suburban commuting.
 India’s First Intermediate Metro car for
Delhi Metro Rail Corporation.
 India’s Biggest 180 Ton (BE1800E)
Excavator, which operates entirely on
electrical power with zero emission.
 India’s Biggest 205 Ton (BH205E)
Dump Truck with environment friendly
Electric Drive Systems.
India’s Biggest 850HP (BD475) Bull
Dozer.
 First proto of Arjun Armored Repair
&amp; Recovery Vehicle (ARRV) to
CVRDE.
Nation’s First state-of-the-art six car
Metro Train set to Kolkata Metro.
Supplied India’s first Driverless Metro
car to Mumbai Metro line 2 & 7.

WHAT ARE SOME OF BEML’S KEY
VALUES AND BELIEFS? HOW IMPORTANT
IS CUSTOMER’S SATISFACTION?
The Company’s core values were christened as
BEML FIRST to Focus on Customer, Innovation
& Technology, Reliability & Quality, Speed, and
Responsiveness and Trust & teamwork:
F — FOCUS ON CUSTOMER;
I — INNOVATION & TECHNOLOGY;
R— RELIABILITY & QUALITY;
S— SPEED & RESPONSIVENESS;
T — TRUST & TEAMWORK;
Comparative study by way of comparing its
products with international manufacturers is a
routine practice to develop the products that
have intense competitive edge. The market is
scanned on a regular basis by the R&amp;D
group in association with the marketing group
to understand, imbibe and develop the
technological innovations being brought out by
the international players.
Proactive innovations are also undertaken in
line with the changing customer expectations.
24/7 service centres created nearer to major
customers is a testimony to the principle of
“Customer First”.
WHAT DO YOU THINK SETS YOUR
BUSINESS APART FROM ITS
COMPETITORS?
The product range, presence at customer pit
head and timely supply with quality products
are the few major factors which make us proud
to be an Indian MNC. I am happy to share that
more than 74% of our sales are being executed
based on the orders received through
competitive bidding. In fact, we have won
India’s biggest single order for supply of
Driverless train for Mumbai Metro line 2 & 7.
Also, we have received trial cum sale order for 1
no. Rope Shovel model BRS-21, which is
under execution.
HOW HAVE YOU FOUND THE
MANUFACTURING INDUSTRY IN INDIA?
With increasing degree of economic
liberalization and opening of market
sectors including Defence to private
sector, a host of multinational companies
who are world leaders have set up their
operations in India. These companies have
the advantages of a global market, better
technology, quality, economies of scale
and higher labour productivity. Also,
several Public Sector Enterprise and
Private sector Companies have
established world-class facilities in order
to promote Atma Nirbhar Bharat
campaign.
However, still lots of enhancement / new
technologies' viz. Industry 4.0, Natural
Language Processing based systems, AI
based automations, etc. are underway
which will make India a perfect
manufacturing hub to meet global
requirements.
